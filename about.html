SWM Project · 2026 · U Lab, UIUC · Research Co-Lead

  • Investigate market-implied belief updates as supervision for aggregate responses to real-world events, including potential transfer to interpreting individuals’ beliefs.

Sign Language Translation with 3D Motion Representations · 2026 · DGene Inc.

  • Investigated SMPL-X representations and 3D motion priors for end-to-end sign language translation, adapting baseline models to test the feasibility of 3D inputs.
  • Designed probing experiments showing that intermediate motion-capture representations retain confidence-related information lost in the final decoded poses.
  • Replaced Uni-Sign’s ST-GCN encoder with a motion-capture-pretrained model: with the same RGB videos and hyperparameters, it reached comparable CSL-News translation performance in fewer training steps.

SymBridge — Human–Robot Interaction · 2024–2025 · Prof. Yuexin Ma’s group, ShanghaiTech

  • Contributed to the design of a human-in-the-loop cyber-physical system for human–robot interaction.
  • Led model evaluation, including task-specific evaluation metrics.
